Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Angza ("we", "our", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains how we handle your data.

Data We Collect

How We Use Your Data

  • To provide and improve the audit service.
  • To respond to your messages and support requests.
  • To monitor usage and prevent abuse (e.g., rate limiting).

Data Sharing

We do not share your personal data or document content with third parties, except as required by law or to protect our legal rights.

Data Retention

  • Uploaded documents are deleted immediately after the audit.
  • Contact form submissions are retained for up to 30 days for record‑keeping and then deleted.

Your Rights
